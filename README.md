# E-Commerce Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce business data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and overall business trends.

The data was cleaned and analyzed using Python, and an interactive dashboard was created using Power BI to present meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Identify important business patterns
- Create meaningful business insights
- Provide recommendations for better business decisions

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Power BI
- Excel/CSV Dataset

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce business information related to customers, products, orders, sales, categories, cities, and payment methods.

The dataset was used to analyze business performance and customer purchasing patterns.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The raw dataset was cleaned using Python and Pandas.

The following data-cleaning steps were performed:

- Checked for missing values
- Removed duplicate records
- Checked and corrected data types
- Handled inconsistent data
- Checked the dataset structure
- Prepared clean data for analysis and visualization

---

## 📊 Data Analysis

The cleaned data was analyzed to identify important business metrics and trends.

The analysis included:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Category-wise Sales
- Product Performance
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Customer Analysis
- City-wise Performance
- Payment Method Analysis

---

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to visualize the results of the analysis.

### Dashboard Includes:

- 💰 Total Revenue
- 🛒 Total Orders
- 👥 Total Customers
- 🏆 Top Category
- 📍 Top City
- 💳 Payment Method Analysis
- 📅 Monthly Sales Trend
- 📦 Category/Product Performance

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. Identified the top-performing product categories based on total sales and revenue.
2. Analyzed monthly sales trends to understand business performance over time.
3. Identified the cities contributing the highest sales and customer activity.
4. Analyzed customer purchasing patterns to understand customer behavior.
5. Identified the most preferred payment methods used by customers.
6. Compared product and category performance to identify high- and low-performing areas.
7. Analyzed overall revenue and order performance to evaluate the business's sales growth and performance.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the identified insights, the following recommendations can be made:

1. Focus more on high-performing products and categories.
2. Maintain sufficient stock of popular products.
3. Use targeted promotions for low-performing categories.
4. Focus marketing efforts on cities with high customer demand.
5. Provide multiple convenient payment options for customers.
6. Monitor monthly sales trends to improve future sales planning.
7. Use customer purchasing patterns to create better offers and promotions.
